How it's calculated

Customs value is item + shipping + insurance (CIF). Duty is charged on that; import VAT is charged on CIF plus duty. Shipping and insurance are for the whole shipment and are split evenly across the units in it; everything below is per unit.

CIF = item + (shipping + insurance) ÷ units Duty = CIF × duty % Import VAT = (CIF + duty) × VAT % Landed = CIF + duty + import VAT (VAT left out if reclaimable) eBay fees = sell price × (FVF % + promoted %) + fixed fee Sale VAT = sell price × VAT % ÷ (100 + VAT %) (only if ticked) Total = landed + UK postage + packaging + eBay fees + sale VAT Profit = sell price − total Margin = profit ÷ sell price

The minimum price solves profit = target margin × price for price:

Min price = (landed + postage + packaging + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − FVF % − promoted % − sale VAT share − target %)
  • Sell price is taken as VAT-inclusive, so the VAT you owe is the VAT fraction of it (1/6 at 20%). Sale VAT share is that fraction, or 0 if unticked.
  • VAT eBay charges on its own fees isn't modelled; if you're VAT registered you can usually reclaim it.
  • For consignments of £135 or less, VAT is normally charged by the seller or marketplace at checkout rather than at import, and customs duty is usually not due.
